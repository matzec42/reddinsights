# reddinsights
An AI-powered analyzer of Reddit threads and subreddits with simple visualizations.

Curious about netizen sentiment about a topic? Simply enter a search term OR a subreddit topic (e.g., /McDonalds) into the analyzer and click the "Give me Reddinsights" button.

You'll receive an AI-powered analysis that will summarize the overall sentiment of the Reddit discussion, keywords that reflect where the discussion is trending, and a handful of targeted insights which can help inform your next actions & business decisions.
